# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Contributors are expected to create a respectful, inclusive and evidence-oriented environment.
Disagreement about model design, fairness, risk or policy should focus on methods and evidence,
not individuals.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and professional language.
- Respect different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Report limitations, uncertainty and adverse findings honestly.
- Protect privacy and never introduce real applicant data.
- Accept constructive review and correct material errors promptly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, disclosure of private information, or manipulation of
analytical evidence is not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove contributions or participation that violate these standards.
