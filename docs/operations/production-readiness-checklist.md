# Production readiness checklist

The current project fails this checklist by design. It is a portfolio demonstration.

## Data and model

- [ ] Representative, lawful and approved real-world dataset
- [ ] Independent data-quality and lineage assessment
- [ ] Outcome definition and performance windows approved
- [ ] Independent model validation completed
- [ ] Reject inference and selection-bias strategy reviewed
- [ ] Calibration, stability and stress testing completed
- [ ] External and temporal validity demonstrated

## Fairness and legal

- [ ] Age-band disparity investigated and resolved or formally governed
- [ ] Intersectional and uncertainty analysis completed
- [ ] Jurisdiction-specific credit and AI legal review completed
- [ ] Disclosure and adverse-action language approved
- [ ] Complaint, appeal and remediation process tested
- [ ] Meaningful human oversight training completed

## Operations and security

- [ ] Identity, mTLS, rate limiting and secret management implemented
- [ ] Artifact signing, SBOM and deployment attestations enabled
- [ ] Immutable model, policy and decision audit records
- [ ] Disaster recovery and manual fallback tested
- [ ] Monitoring thresholds approved and target labels operational
- [ ] Incident and rollback exercise passed

## Governance

- [ ] Model owner, policy owner and validation owner formally assigned
- [ ] Change approval and segregation of duties operating
- [ ] Risk acceptance signed by authorized committee
- [ ] Controlled pilot exit criteria approved
